Background & Engineering Journey

I'm Anmol Bakshi, a Software Engineer specializing in Cloud Platform Architecture, DevSecOps, Applied AI, and Distributed Systems. I hold a Bachelor of Technology in Computer Science & Engineering from Dr. B. R. Ambedkar National Institute of Technology (NIT) Jalandhar, and currently build secure, enterprise-scale healthcare platforms at Optum.

Over 2+ years of enterprise engineering, I have taken production ownership across distributed architectures consisting of 24 microservices powered by Microsoft Azure and Azure Service Bus. My focus lies at the intersection of robust backend engineering, infrastructure-as-code automation, automated secret lifecycle management, and applied artificial intelligence.

I believe great software is not just functional, but observable, resilient to failure, automated by default, and secured from day zero. Whether eliminating long-lived credentials via OIDC workload identity federation, remediating 100+ vulnerability findings across multiple teams, or driving MTTR down by ~75% through telemetry-driven alerting, I take pride in delivering measurable engineering impact.
